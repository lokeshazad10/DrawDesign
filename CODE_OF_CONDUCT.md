# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making DrawDesign a welcoming, respectful community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Help maintain a safe, inclusive environment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention,
publishing another person's private information, and any other conduct that would reasonably
make the community unsafe or unwelcoming are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy privately to **[maintainer contact placeholder]**.
Include relevant details and links, and avoid publicizing private reports. Maintainers will
review reports promptly, keep them confidential to the extent practical, and may remove content,
issue a warning, or restrict or ban participation depending on the circumstances. Retaliation
against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
